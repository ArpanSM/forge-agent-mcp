# Three directions — internal, one chosen

All three use the real console captures and the product's own palette. The film is
30 s, 1920×1080, music and effects only (no narration).

## A · The Gate
Language: modular-grid + cursor-demo, with kinetic type interruptions.
Look: the real review console as the world; cream paper, dark-green ink, one amber gate.
Palette: cream `#f7f3e9` 55% · green `#173f38` 27% · navy `#142235` 10% · amber `#b45309` 8% — from the existing end card and the console's own CSS variables.
Logline: The agent reads all 16 cases, cites 45 fields and recommends approval — then stops at a physical amber gate until a human mints the token; the write lands and the audit chain verifies.
Why it fits: the message shape is a mechanism with an evidence payoff; the personality is precise, technical, high-stakes and new, so a system diagram cut from real UI is the honest language.
Feel: hybrid; 120 BPM; 3–6 events per bar; the first 3 s show the real 16-case queue with "16 cases" / "2 need a human"; a stop at 12 s; the drop is the human click at 14 s; a 5-cut proof burst at 21 s; end card at 24 s.
Risk: UI text can get too small at feed size; the gate must be a real system boundary, not a decorative bar.

## B · Every Value Has a Page
Language: editorial-collage + data-story type.
Look: navy field, cream evidence cards, macro citation rows, amber bbox.
Palette: navy `#142235` 58% · cream `#f7f3e9` 32% · amber `#b45309` 7% · green `#2f7d63` 3% — the console's navy and end-card cream, with the amber of the pending/hold state.
Logline: One field at a time, every number on screen is shown to be a page, a bounding box and a verbatim quote; the recommendation is only the last line of the evidence.
Why it fits: the product's differentiator is cited extraction; type-led evidence is the clearest possible expression of "no invented values".
Feel: montage; 120 BPM; cuts on values; first 3 s macro on a citation row; a dense evidence burst at 40%.
Risk: it becomes a type poster and never stages the human decision or the audited write.

![B · Every Value Has a Page](b-cited-0.00s.png)

## C · The Packet
Language: 3d-object / interface-in-space (the one depth direction).
Look: a dark studio, five document cards as physical objects, the real console mounted as a screen, an amber approval seal in front.
Palette: navy `#142235` 62% · cream `#f7f3e9` 24% · amber `#b45309` 10% · green `#173f38` 4% — the console's navy plus the end-card palette as physical materials.
Logline: The packet is a physical stack of paper; the camera moves through the five documents and stops at a seal that only a human can press.
Why it fits: a premium launch treatment for a product whose evidence is literally paper; makes the documents tangible.
Feel: continuous; 108 BPM; one camera move; first 3 s orbit across the packet; the seal lands on the drop.
Risk: the "interface in space" look is common, and the object metaphor says "documents" but not "the agent cannot approve itself".

![C · The Packet](c-packet-0.00s.png)

## Chosen: A · The Gate

A is the only direction whose central object *is* the rule: the recommendation reaches a
boundary it cannot cross. It carries the three required real UI moments (queue → cited
Ridgeline fields/recommendation → committed decision/audit chain) on one continuous
spine, and the human click is a real payoff rather than a caption. B is the best pure
evidence frame and will be reused as the citation insert in A's second act; C's paper
stack is a strong image but would make the film about documents rather than the gate.
