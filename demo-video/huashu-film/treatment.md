# Treatment — The Gate

**Chosen direction:** A · The Gate. 30 s · 1920×1080 · 120 BPM · hybrid route.
**One idea:** a recommendation can travel through the whole system and still cannot pass
the last inch; a human decision mints the token, and the audited write is the payoff.

## Music

World: minimal techno with a marimba/piano motif — precise, infrastructural, human at the
edge. 120 BPM, A minor, 15 bars. The motif (A–C–E–D) is stated on marimba in the hook,
doubled by piano at the drop, and becomes the end-card button. The stop at bar 7 is a tape
stop; the drop at bar 8 lands on the human click; the end is a button on bar 13 with a ring.
